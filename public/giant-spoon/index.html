Budgets, vendors, concurrent jobs.

The Senior Producer seat asks for someone who can bid a job, hold a vendor, and keep several productions honest at the same time. NYC hybrid — Greater New York, Tuesday through Thursday on site.

Integrated production

  • Lead broadcast, digital, social, experiential, and branded-content jobs from concept through delivery.
  • Partner with Creative on what is executable before the idea hardens.
  • Work with Account on client timing and with Business Affairs on talent, music, and contracts.
  • Mentor associate and junior producers without handing them the board.

Cost and partners

  • Build, track, and reconcile production budgets.
  • Bid and negotiate directors, production companies, editorial, VFX, and finish partners.
  • Keep 6–8 concurrent campaigns visible: owners, dates, and risk.
  • Present plans and numbers without hiding the tradeoffs.
