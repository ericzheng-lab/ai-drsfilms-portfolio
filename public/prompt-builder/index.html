04How it works

Pick what looks right.
The tool writes the syntax.

You never need to memorize a single parameter. Choose from film vocabulary — the prompt assembles itself, formatted for the model you pick.

+epic wide shot +35mm anamorphic +low-angle dusk light +awe-struck and foreboding +Villeneuve grandeur +2.39:1
/imagine prompt: monumental brutalist steampunk cityscape, modern man alone on a windswept hill, epic wide shot, 35mm anamorphic, low-angle dusk light, teal-and-amber industrial haze, awe-struck and foreboding, Villeneuve grandeur, Ridley Scott industrial worldbuilding, volumetric smoke --ar 2.39:1 --style raw

Underlined tokens come straight from your selections. Everything else — ordering, separators, parameters — is assembled by the tool.

08BYOK — your key, your data

Works offline.
No account. No setup.

The whole preset library ships in one HTML file. Add an API key if you want AI to enhance your prompts further — but great results don't need it.

  • Try without a key — add ?mock=1 to the URL for canned replies and a portfolio demo. Open demo mode →
  • Your key stays yours — a static page with no backend: prompts assemble in your browser, and your key is only ever sent to the model provider you choose.
  • English & Chinese — with a key, describe your shot to the agent in either language; it returns a model-ready prompt either way.
