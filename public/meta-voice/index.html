How I produce voice

A disciplined route from casting brief to approved performance, implementation and delivery.

01 / BRIEF

Define the voice need

Audience, product context, language, persona, scripts and usage boundaries.

02 / CAST

Build the casting brief

Performance range, accent or dialect, references, availability and rights scope.

03 / DIRECT

Shape the performance

Direct talent or synthetic voice toward intention, timing, pronunciation and consistency.

04 / RECORD

Run the session

Capture, monitor, document and protect usable takes across studio or remote workflows.

05 / EDIT

Edit, conform & QC

Select, clean, time, version and review the voice against picture or product behavior.

06 / DELIVER

Close rights & delivery

Approved assets, naming, versions, consent records and accountable handoff.

Voice production contract

Performance, rights and product behavior before output.

  • Brief first: define persona, audience, language, usage and delivery context.
  • Direction: shape intention, pacing, pronunciation and continuity rather than accepting raw output.
  • Editorial fit: review voice against picture, sound design or product behavior and repair timing in post.
  • Rights: treat voice likeness, AI usage, consent and privacy as production decisions, not settings.
  • Boundary: non-union experience only; no claim of union-agreement or large-scale voice-library ownership.

Operating rule

Method follows the production need

  • Choose traditional, AI-assisted or hybrid methods by craft and delivery risk
  • Provider success never equals creative acceptance
  • Repair continuity and audio problems rather than hiding them
  • Escalate brand, rights and accuracy uncertainty to a person
  • Voice likeness, AI-usage consent and privacy stay inside human approval
  • Release only after accountable human sign-off
