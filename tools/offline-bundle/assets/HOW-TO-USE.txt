LITTLE FILMMAKERS - WEEK 1 PRESENTER DECK
Offline copy. Runs with the Wi-Fi off.
==========================================================

TO START IT
-----------
Double-click  START-THE-DECK.html
It opens in your browser. That is the whole thing. Nothing to install,
no password, no terminal.


TO PUT IT ON THE CLASSROOM LAPTOP
---------------------------------
Copy the WHOLE folder - not just the START-THE-DECK file. The pictures
and the five old films live in the "media" folder beside it, and the deck
is blank without them.

The folder is about 40 MB. That is small enough for AirDrop, a USB stick,
or a Google Drive download. Three ways, easiest first:

  1. AirDrop the folder from your Mac to the classroom Mac.
  2. Drag the folder onto a USB stick, plug it into the laptop, then drag
     it OFF the stick onto the Desktop. Do not run it from the stick.
  3. Zip it, upload it to Drive, download and unzip on the laptop.

Then double-click START-THE-DECK.html on that laptop, and step through a
few screens before the children arrive.


WHAT WORKS WITH NO NETWORK
--------------------------
  - All 32 screens, correct type, correct colours
  - Every photograph and every gallery, full size
  - The five old films play: Lumiere x3, Melies, Steamboat Willie
    (that is minutes 43-60, the whole history section)
  - Presenter notes, the segment clock, the overview grid, the lightbox


WHAT STILL NEEDS A CONNECTION
-----------------------------
Eight clips stream from YouTube: Paddington, The Lion King (1994),
Shaun the Sheep, the BBC chameleon, the Lion King 2019 trailer, Wonka,
and the two optional ones (LEGO, Elmo).

With no connection you still see the correct picture for each one, plus a
line telling you what to do. The deck checks for itself whether YouTube
can be reached, so it behaves correctly even on a school Wi-Fi that is
switched on but has no internet behind it. Two ways round it:

  1. Phone hotspot. Only these eight clips need it, so it is a few
     minutes of video, not a whole website.
  2. Skip them. Every category still shows five films as pictures in its
     gallery. The lesson gets shorter; it does not fall apart.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
The headlines should be a chunky rounded face, not Times or Helvetica.
If the type looks like a plain word processor, the file was copied without
its folder - go back and copy the whole folder.

If a picture is a grey box, same cause.


KEYS
----
  Right arrow / Space   next screen
  Left arrow            back
  N                     presenter notes on and off
  Enter                 play the clip on this screen
  1 - 6                 the six clips in the guessing game
  Esc                   close a clip or a picture;
                        with nothing open, shows the grid of all 32 screens
